Share the Data, Not the People

Synthetic copies of three public datasets of real people, made six ways with and without differential privacy, then attacked the way a regulator says (singling out, linkability, inference) and the way a researcher would (membership inference). Pick a dataset and a synthesiser to see what each privacy budget costs in usefulness and buys in risk. Every point carries its 95% interval; nothing here is modelled in the browser.

What leaked

A risk counts as a leak when its interval, at the Bonferroni-corrected level the recommendation rule uses, excludes zero. Each is a property of that library, version and configuration on this data.
